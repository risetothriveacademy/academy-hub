# Rise to Thrive Retreats — Master Workflow, Launch & Ads

**Status:** Agreed strategic reference, November 18, 2026. Use to align Diane, Aaron, Claude, and the VA team. Planning framework, not confirmation that campaigns or integrations are live.

## Canonical S1–S5 vocabulary

- **S1 — WHO AM I? (Audience):** Individuals, entrepreneurs, business owners, professionals; or helping professionals and organizations.
- **S2 — WHAT HAPPENED TO ME / WHAT AM I STRUGGLING WITH? (Recognizable experience):** Twelve everyday-language challenges: Trauma; Abuse; Divorce; Betrayal; Financial Collapse; Burnout; Anxiety; Depression; Identity Loss; Grief; Isolation; Shame. These include events, conditions, symptoms, and emotional struggles—not twelve uniform root causes.
- **S3 — HOW IS IT AFFECTING MY LIFE? (Impact):** Mental, emotional, relational, professional, financial, and spiritual effects; confidence, decisions, purpose and livelihood.
- **S4 — WHAT IS KEEPING ME STUCK? (Contributing factors / root-cause exploration):** Explore patterns and circumstances potentially contributing to difficulty moving forward, using the 18 mini-course topics. No diagnostic claim or automatic one-to-one match.
- **S5 — HOW DO I MOVE FORWARD? (Pathway):** Free resources; Foundation Course; relevant Mini-Courses; Retreat; small-group Mastermind (proposed limit 10); Advanced Mastery premium 1:1 support.

**Core principle:** S2 is the experience someone recognizes, S4 explores factors that may keep them stuck, and S5 proposes suitable next steps.

## Two retreat branches

### Branch A — Individuals / Personal and Business Recovery

- Individuals, entrepreneurs, business owners, and professionals experiencing effects of adversity on life, work, finances, relationships, confidence, and purpose.
- Message: reconnect with resilience, rebuild personal and business capacity, and develop a practical path forward. Do not promise clinical trauma treatment or financial restoration.
- Funnel: S1 → S2 → S3 → S4 → S5 → audience-specific HeyGen ad/content → Individual Retreat landing page → inquiry/application → GoHighLevel follow-up → suitable retreat enrollment, or course alternative.
- Proposed retreat participant package: Foundation Course, two self-selected root-cause mini-courses, and six months of devotional access, subject to final published terms. Do not resell the included courses immediately after enrollment.
- The Devotional App is separately operated by Hope When Broken; confirm organizational permission and access arrangements before bundling.

### Branch B — Helping Professionals / Expansion

- Therapists, counselors, coaches, social workers, nurses/healthcare professionals, recovery leaders, ministry leaders, chaplains, educators, NGOs and HR/organizational leaders.
- Message: expand reach, resources, services and impact; respond to challenges experienced by people they serve.
- Funnel: profession → clients'/community's recognizable challenges → profession-specific HeyGen ad → Professional Retreat landing page → inquiry/application → CRM and VA follow-up → professional retreat and optional later development pathway.
- Future Directory/referral/facilitator roles require qualification checks, separate training and approval. Retreat participation alone is not professional certification.

## Advertising and lead routing

- HeyGen library contains audience-specific videos, but no verified evidence of **live paid ads**. Check Ads Manager campaign statuses, spend and results.
- Start with three groups per branch: **A:** Business Resilience, Career & Identity, Life After Adversity. **B:** Therapists/Coaches, Healthcare/HR, Ministry/NGOs.

- Record campaign, video asset, audience, interest, source, status, owner, due date, approval, and outcome in GoHighLevel.
- Automated calendar prompts require configured and tested workflows. No unauthorized outbound materials.

## From inquiry to long-term relationship

1. Recognize S2 experience and S3 impact through relevant content.
2. See branch-specific ad; visit landing page and explore appropriate S4/S5 education.
3. Submit inquiry; VA records and arranges suitable discovery/approval.
4. Enroll in retreat if suitable, or choose Foundation/Mini-Course learning pathway.
5. Retreat participants use included learning access; optional separate devotional access if arranged.
6. Offer a suitable small-group **Mastermind** (proposed cap 10), based on readiness.
7. Offer suitable **Advanced Mastery** 1:1 to support stabilization, rebuilding and scaling following adversity.
8. Helping professionals may apply for directory/referral/collaboration after vetting and training.
